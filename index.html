I was born and raised in Nepal, where I earned both my Bachelor and Master of Science degrees in Physics from Tribhuvan University. In 2019, I obtained a Master of Science in Physics from The University of Texas at El Paso (UTEP). In December 2024, I received my Ph.D. in Computational Science from UTEP, where I was honored with the Academic & Research Excellence Outstanding Graduate Student Award.

My research focuses on modeling the free energy and thermal properties of solids—particularly transition metals—under extreme pressures and temperatures. This work aims to develop more accurate methods for evaluating thermal properties and equations of state (EOS). Temperature effects are incorporated using Harmonic Ensemble Lattice Dynamics (HELD) through classical molecular dynamics (LAMMPS) and quantum molecular dynamics (QMD).
