About Me Teaching

I was born and raised in Nepal, where I earned a Bachelor of Science and a Master of Science in Physics from Tribhuvan University.

In 2019, I obtained a Master of Science in Physics from The University of Texas at El Paso (UTEP). I received my Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) degree in Computational Science from UTEP in December 2024 with the Academic & Research Excellence Outstanding Graduate Student Computational Science award.

My research focuses on computational physics and materials science, particularly the thermal properties, lattice dynamics, free energy, and equations of state of transition metals and alloys under high pressures and temperatures.

My work uses first-principles calculations, density functional theory, molecular dynamics, lattice dynamics, and computational methods to understand material properties at finite temperatures.

Research Interests

  • Density Functional Theory
  • Molecular Dynamics
  • Lattice Dynamics and Phonons
  • Thermal Properties of Materials

Teaching

I am currently a Professor of Physics at Central Texas College. My teaching focuses on helping students understand fundamental physics concepts through lectures, problem solving, laboratory experiments, demonstrations, and real-world applications.

I emphasize conceptual understanding together with mathematical problem-solving skills and encourage students to connect physics principles with everyday physical phenomena.
